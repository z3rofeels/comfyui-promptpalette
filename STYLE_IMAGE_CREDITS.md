Published explorer render assets are supplied by the MIT-licensed [Anima Assets ZVX mirror](https://github.com/vZVXv/Anima-Assets-ZVX-Edition). Copyright (c) 2026 ThetaCursed. These are model interpretations labeled by the source explorer, not original artwork by the named artists. Only reviewed nonsexual portrait excerpts are distributed. The exact crop, source commit, original checksum and resulting checksum appear in `web/assets/styles/manifest.json`. Source checkpoint, seed and generation recipe are not claimed.
